About Sahil Building Material

Building Rohini's Landmarks with Unwavering Trust

Located conveniently on 4th Road, Pocket 6 in Rohini Sector 6D, Sahil Building Material has been the cornerstone supplier for residential houses, builder floors, and commercial infrastructure across Delhi NCR for more than two decades.

Established in 2002, Sahil Building Material was founded with a singular, clear vision: to provide contractors, architects, builders, and individual home builders in Rohini with direct access to genuine, factory-fresh construction materials at honest wholesale prices.

In an industry where adulteration, underweight deliveries, and delayed dispatch can ruin project schedules, we built our reputation on strict transparency. Whether you require a single truck of Yamuna river sand or 500 metric tons of certified Tata Tiscon TMT bars, every order is backed by genuine manufacturer test certificates and guaranteed accurate weighment.
